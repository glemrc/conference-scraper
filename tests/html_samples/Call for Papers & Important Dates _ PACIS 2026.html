CALL FOR PAPERS & IMPORTANT DATES

Program Co-Chairs

  • Shuk Ying (Susanna) Ho Australian National University
  • Chee Wei (David) Phang University of Nottingham Ningbo China
  • Chuan-Hoo Tan National University of Singapore

PACIS 2026 stands at the forefront of a significant and transformational shift in the Asia-Pacific region towards a digital economy, where seamless exchanges transcend national and societal boundaries. This evolution is driven by a range of technological innovations, particularly the rapid advancements in artificial intelligence. Yet, as we navigate this exciting landscape, we also face a world marked by division and emerging challenges that bring about swift political, economic, and societal changes. At this pivotal moment, PACIS 2026 embraces the inspiring theme of “Leapfrogging the Future with Artificial Intelligence”, encapsulated through eighteen tracks that reflect a comprehensive perspective on this groundbreaking journey.

We graciously invite you to participate in an engaging in-person discussion from July 4th to 8th, 2026, in Jakarta, Indonesia. This gathering will focus on the vital role of information systems research in exploring how various technologies, including artificial intelligence, can propel us into the future and foster a more inclusive and equitable digital era. Your insights and contributions will be invaluable as we navigate these important discussions.
