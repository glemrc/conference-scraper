Call for Papers 会议征稿

ICET 2026 is a scientific conference addressing real topics as seen by teachers, students, parents, and school leaders. Both scientists, professionals, and institutional leaders are invited to be informed by experts, sharpen their understanding of what education needs, and explore how to achieve it. The topics include but are not limited to the following:


Track 1: Inclusive Learning Environments

Universal Design for Learning (UDL) in Digital Education
Accessibility Features in EdTech Tools for Diverse Learners
Strategies for Addressing the Digital Divide
Promoting Equity in Online and Hybrid Learning Models
EdTech for Supporting Students with Disabilities
Culturally Responsive Educational Technologies
Gender Inclusivity in Educational Technology Adoption
Gamification to Foster Engagement for Marginalized Groups

Track 2: Artificial Intelligence in Education

AI-Powered Personalized Learning Systems
Chatbots and Virtual Assistants in Student Support Services
Ethical Considerations in AI for Education
Adaptive Learning Platforms for Tailored Instruction
Automated Assessment and Feedback Systems
Leveraging AI for Predictive Analytics in Education
Language Processing Tools for Multilingual Learning Environments
Using AI to Enhance Teacher Professional Development

Track 3: Emerging Technologies in Learning

Virtual Reality (VR) and Augmented Reality (AR) in Immersive Learning
Blockchain Applications for Academic Credentialing
Wearable Technology for Data-Driven Learning Insights
Internet of Things (IoT) in Smart Classrooms
Cloud-Based Solutions for Scalable Education Delivery
Haptic Technologies for Hands-On Learning Experiences
Serious Games and Simulations for STEM Education
5G Networks and Their Impact on Mobile Learning

Track 4: Technology-Enabled Cultural Inheritance

Intangible Cultural Heritage (ICH) Teaching and Inheritance in Digital Technology
VR/AR Educational Applications for Cultural Heritage
Intelligent Learning Tools for Traditional Languages
Integration of Digital Museums with Classroom Teaching
Personalized Recommendation Technology for Cultural Courses
Virtual Simulation Training for Folk Culture
Application of Educational Big Data in Cultural Inheritance
Digital Teaching Programs for Traditional Crafts

Track 5: Technology-Enabled Artistic Creation

AI Collaborative Creation in Art Education
Digital Painting and 3D Modeling Teaching Tools
Intelligent Auxiliary Systems for Music Creation
Technical Construction of Immersive Art Classrooms
Virtual Simulation Design for Stage Art
Digital Copyright Protection for Artistic Creations
Integration of Interactive Media with Art Teaching
Digital Exhibition of Students' Artworks

Track 6: Technology-Enabled Innovative Talent Cultivation

Digital Collaboration Platforms for Interdisciplinary Learning
Technical Tool Integration in STEAM Education
Programming Education and Computational Thinking Cultivation
Innovative Applications of Virtual Simulation Experiments
Digital Incubation Support for Innovation and Entrepreneurship Projects
Technology-Enabled Programs for Project-Based Learning (PBL)
Digital Skill Enhancement Tools for Lifelong Learning
AI Educational Applications for Innovative Thinking Cultivation
