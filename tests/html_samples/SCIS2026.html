Call for Papers

The University of Canberra, Australia and Soft Computing Research Society will organize the 3rd International Conference on Sustainable Computing and Intelligent Systems (SCIS 2026)  during November 21-22, 2026. 

 

Submissions

  • Full-length paper: Accepted papers will be published in the Scopus Indexed Springer Book Series "Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems" (Approval Pending).
  • Abstract Only: Authors will be given the chance to present during the conference. 

The topics covered (but are not limited to) in the conference are as follows:

Sustainable Computing:

Green Computing, Energy-efficient algorithms and software design, Low-power hardware design, Energy-efficient data centers and cloud computing, Renewable Energy Integration, energy-aware computing, Sustainable materials in hardware manufacturing, Recycling and responsible disposal of electronic waste, Energy-efficient communication protocols,    Green networking technologies, Environmental impact assessment of computing systems, Sustainable product life cycle management, Intelligent Resource Management, Dynamic resource allocation for energy efficiency,  Adaptive and self-aware systems, Intelligent systems for energy-efficient buildings, Sustainable urban computing, IoT and Sustainability, Energy-efficient IoT devices and network, Sustainable IoT applications, Data Analytics for Sustainability, Data-driven approaches for environmental monitoring, Sustainable data storage and processing, Reuse and recycling of computing hardware, Circular design principles for software and hardware, Strategies for reducing the carbon footprint of computing activities, Government policies and regulations promoting sustainable computing, Compliance and standards for eco-friendly computing

 

Intelligent Systems (Hardware & Software):

Internet of Things, Information Security, Embedded Systems, Real-time Systems, Cloud Computing, Big Data Analysis, Quantum Computing, Automation Systems, Intelligent IoT eHealth, Bio-Inspired Intelligence, Brain Modeling and Simulation, Cognitive Systems, Cyber-Physical Systems, Data Analytics, Data/Web Mining, Data Science, Hybrid Systems, Intelligence for Security, Intelligent Decision Making Systems, Intelligent Information Processing, Intelligent Transportation, Artificial Intelligence for Machine Vision, Imaging Sensors Technology, Features Extraction, Color and Texture Analysis, Image Segmentation, Convolutional Neural Network, Biometrics Recognition, Biomedical Imaging, Image/Video Classification, Image Restoration, Soft Computing for Machine Vision, Pattern Recognition, Gesture Recognition, Action Recognition, Intelligent Transport Systems, Surveillance, Human-Computer Interaction

All submissions need to be submitted through the CMT using the link:

https://cmt3.research.microsoft.com/SCIS2026
